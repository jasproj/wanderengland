Types of Tours

Historic Pub Walk

Visit the oldest, quirkiest, and most storied pubs in the city. Hear about literary regulars, Dickensian snugs, and the pub where the Great Fire of London was plotted.

Craft Beer Trail

Tap rooms, microbreweries, and bottle shops serving everything from hazy IPAs to barrel-aged stouts. London, Manchester, and Bristol lead the scene.

Brewery Tour

Go behind the scenes at working breweries. See the mash tun, smell the hops, and taste straight from the conditioning tank.

Traditional Ale Tasting

Learn the difference between bitter, mild, porter, and stout. Real ale from cask — hand-pulled, naturally carbonated, and served at cellar temperature.

Where to Go

The City & Fleet Street

London

Ye Olde Cheshire Cheese (1667), The Blackfriar (Arts & Crafts interior), and the pubs where journalists and barristers have drunk for centuries.

Southwark

London

The George Inn (the last galleried coaching inn in London), The Anchor (where Pepys watched the Great Fire), and Borough Market beer stalls.

Northern Quarter

Manchester

The epicentre of Manchester's craft beer scene. Cloudwater, Track, and Marble Brewery tap rooms within walking distance.

The Shambles

York

Medieval streets lined with traditional pubs. The Guy Fawkes Inn sits on the street where the plotter was born.

Bristol Harbour

Bristol

Left Handed Giant, Moor Beer, and the oldest pub in Bristol (The Hatchet, 1606). A thriving craft scene on the harbour waterfront.

Cambridge Backs

Cambridge

The Eagle (where Crick and Watson announced DNA's structure), The Anchor, and riverside pubs overlooking King's College.

Tips

Pace Yourself

English pints are 568 ml — larger than US pints. Halves are perfectly acceptable and let you try more beers across the tour.

Eat Before You Go

Most pub tours include tasting pours but not food. Line your stomach — a ploughman's lunch or a pie does the job.

Cash for Tips

Card payments are universal, but a cash tip for your guide (5-10 GBP) saves them the processing fee.

Last Orders

Pubs close at 11 PM on weeknights, midnight on weekends. Plan your evening — 'time at the bar' is called 20 minutes before close.
