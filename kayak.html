Types of Kayak

🌊 Sea Kayak

Coastal paddling — caves, cliffs, and protected bays. Single or tandem, guided or rental.

🏞️ River / Lake Kayak

Inland calm-water paddling. Easier conditions and a different set of landscapes than coastal sea kayak.

🤿 Kayak + Snorkel

Combined trips that paddle to remote snorkel sites. Gear stowed in dry hatches; longer half-day format.

Best Kayak Spots

River Thames (Richmond to Hampton Court)

West London

Classic suburban Thames route past Hampton Court Palace. Multiple operator put-ins, calm water year-round.

Lake District (Derwentwater)

Cumbria

Open-water paddling under Skiddaw with multiple island stops. Operators in Keswick.

Cornwall coast (Falmouth)

South-west

Sea-kayak the Helford River and out to St. Mawes. Coastal cave exploration on calm days.

Norfolk Broads

East England

200km of navigable inland waterways. Multi-day kayak-camping or single-day rentals from Wroxham.

Symonds Yat (River Wye)

Forest of Dean / Wye Valley

One of England's most scenic stretches of river — limestone cliffs, peregrine falcons, calm flatwater.

Lundy Island (from Bideford)

Devon

Sea-kayak day trips to Lundy with seal encounters and basalt cliff caves. Advanced paddlers.

Kayak Tips

🌅 Go Early

Mornings offer the calmest conditions. Afternoon wind makes paddling harder.

🧴 Sun Protection

You're exposed on the water. Hat, sunscreen, and rash guard. Reapply often.

💧 Waterproof Bag

Bring a dry bag for phone, snacks, and extra clothes. Most tours provide them.

👟 Water Shoes

You'll be getting in and out on rocky shores. Reef-safe water shoes protect feet.
