Types of Tours

Market & Street Food Walk

Borough Market, Camden Market, and Maltby Street — sample artisan cheeses, fresh oysters, scotch eggs, and sourdough from the stalls that started it all.

Afternoon Tea Experience

Finger sandwiches, scones with clotted cream, and a tiered cake stand. Classic, modern, and themed versions across London's finest hotels and tearooms.

Pub Grub & Ale Tour

Fish and chips, pies, Sunday roasts, and real ales in historic pubs. Learn why the pub is England's living room.

Multicultural Food Walk

Brick Lane curries, Chinatown dim sum, and Brixton's Caribbean kitchens. London's immigrant communities built one of the world's great food cities.

Where to Go

Borough Market

London

England's oldest food market (since 1014). Over 100 stalls selling everything from aged cheddar to Cornish pasties to Ethiopian injera.

Soho

London

Dim sum on Gerrard Street, Italian delis on Old Compton Street, and Michelin stars tucked into narrow Georgian townhouses.

Balti Triangle

Birmingham

The birthplace of the balti — over 50 curry houses in a half-mile radius. Best experienced after 8 PM when the kitchens hit full stride.

The Shambles

York

Medieval street now lined with independent food shops, chocolatiers, and fudge makers. The narrowest point is barely a metre wide.

Cotswolds

Gloucestershire

Farm shops, creameries, and country pubs serving locally reared lamb and Gloucestershire Old Spots pork.

Brighton Lanes

East Sussex

Independent cafes, vegan restaurants, and seafood shacks along the coast. The freshest fish and chips in the south.

Tips

Come Hungry

Food tours include 6-10 tastings. Eat a light breakfast — you'll be full by the halfway point.

Mention Allergies Early

Tell your guide about dietary requirements when booking. Most tours can accommodate gluten-free, vegetarian, and halal needs.

Tipping at Markets

Market stalls don't expect tips, but guided food tours typically suggest 10-15% if the guide was excellent.

Book Weekday Mornings

Borough Market and other popular spots are heaving on Saturdays. Tuesday-Friday mornings give you space to actually taste things.
