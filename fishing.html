Types of Fishing

🎣 Inshore / Light Tackle

Half-day shallow-water fishing. Snapper, jacks, and reef species. Family-friendly.

🐟 Offshore / Deep Sea

Full-day blue-water trolling for marlin, mahi, tuna, wahoo. Bigger boats, longer runs.

🏞️ Freshwater / River

Trout, salmon, pike, or coarse fishing depending on region. Wading or boat-based.

Best Fishing Spots

River Test (Hampshire)

South England

Iconic chalk-stream trout fishing. Day permits and lessons available year-round.

Lake District (Bassenthwaite, Coniston)

Cumbria

Pike, perch, and trout in glacial lakes. Boat hire and bank-fishing both common.

Norfolk Broads

East England

Bream, roach, perch in connected waterway system. Multi-day boat-fishing possible.

Cornwall (sea fishing from Falmouth/Mevagissey)

South-west

Mackerel, bass, pollack on charter day-boats. Half-day options from harbours.

River Severn (Worcester/Shropshire)

West Midlands

Barbel, chub, pike — coarse-fishing classic. Day tickets from local clubs.

Yorkshire trout streams (Wharfe, Nidd)

Yorkshire

Wild brown trout and grayling. Best March-October.

Fishing Tips

🌅 Dawn departures

Most charters leave before sunrise. Plan your sleep accordingly.

🤢 Sea-sickness meds

Even half-day inshore can rough up — take meds the night before.

💵 Tip the mate

Crew is partly tip-funded. 15-20% of charter cost is standard.

🐟 Catch policy

Confirm catch-and-release vs. take-home before booking. Some species are protected.
