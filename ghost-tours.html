Types of Tours

Classic Ghost Walk

Lantern-lit evening tours through cobbled streets and dark alleyways. Guides blend verified history with spine-tingling tales of hauntings and murders.

Jack the Ripper Tour

Retrace the Whitechapel murders of 1888 through the East End streets where they happened. The most popular themed walk in London.

Haunted Pub Crawl

Visit 3-4 pubs with documented ghost sightings. Hear the stories, then decide for yourself — over a pint in the very room it happened.

Castle & Dungeon Tour

Explore underground passages, former torture chambers, and haunted keeps. York Dungeon and London Dungeon add theatrical thrills.

Where to Go

York

Yorkshire

England's most haunted city. The Shambles, Holy Trinity Church, and the Treasurer's House all have well-documented ghost sightings stretching back centuries.

City of London

London

The Tower of London (Anne Boleyn's ghost), Bank of England (the Black Nun), and dozens of plague-era hauntings in the Square Mile.

Whitechapel

London

Jack the Ripper's East End. The narrow streets, pubs, and yards where five murders took place in 1888 are largely unchanged.

Canterbury

Kent

Archbishop Becket's murder in 1170, plague burials, and the medieval underworld beneath the streets. After-dark tours run year-round.

Chester

Cheshire

Roman fortress city with 2,000 years of hauntings. The most haunted pub in England (Ye Olde Boot Inn) is here.

Stratford-upon-Avon

Warwickshire

Shakespeare's birthplace has its own ghosts. Tudor buildings, the Guild Chapel, and the graveyard where the Bard himself asked to be left undisturbed.

Tips

Book Evening Slots

Ghost tours work best after sunset. Most run at 7-8 PM in winter, 8-9 PM in summer. The atmosphere is half the experience.

Dress Warm

You'll be standing still outdoors for 90 minutes. Even summer evenings in England can be chilly — bring a jacket.

Not Suitable for Young Children

Most ghost tours include graphic historical content (executions, plague, murders). Check the age recommendation before booking for families.

Photography Welcome

Guides usually encourage photos — you might catch an orb or unexplained shadow. Flash is fine outdoors but ask in indoor venues.
